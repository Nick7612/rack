How to play

Controls

  • Move the pointer to aim; the guide shows the object ball's path.
  • Press and drag away to set power, then release to shoot.
  • Arrow keys aim, Up/Down set power, Space shoots.
  • On a foul, click a legal spot to place the cue ball.

8-Ball

  • The break spreads the rack; the table is open until the first legal pot.
  • Pot a ball to claim solids or stripes, then clear your group.
  • Pot the 8-ball last to win. Pot it early or with a foul and you lose.
  • Fouls give the other player ball in hand.

9-Ball

  • Always hit the lowest-numbered ball first.
  • Any legally pocketed ball keeps you at the table.
  • Pot the 9-ball legally — at any time — to win.
  • Fouls give ball in hand; a fouled 9-ball is spotted.
